How it works

Squads still fight their own fights. The commander keeps the whole team pushing for the same objective, and the zone scores on bodies inside it.

A commander per team

Want to lead? Pick Commander in Discord. Our bot offers the job to the best rated player in the pool, and to someone at random if the pool is empty.

One voice per team

You join your team's voice channel and hear the calls. Only the commander talks there, so the plan stays clear.

You report, they decide

Spot something? Say where and what in TEAM chat. Right-click the map to post exact coordinates.

Rate your commander

After each match you get a DM with three buttons: good calls, poor, or toxic. Good ratings put commanders at the front of the line.

How to join

Three steps. You only do them once.

  1. 1

    Join our Discord

    Everything runs through it: voice, commanders, ratings.

    Join Discord
  2. 2

    Join the server

    In WARDOGS, open Community servers and search {{server.search}}, or use Join by ID with the Server ID from #server-info. Stay in the match for step 3.

  3. 3

    Verify

    While you're in the game, type /verify and your in-game name in #get-verified. A code arrives as a private message in game. Type /confirm with it.
