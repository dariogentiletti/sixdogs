HOW WE PLAY · 1/5

Play asa team.

At SIXDOGS we want a game mode that rewards teamwork. When the whole team goes for the same objective, the game is more fun for everyone.

Every team has a commander. The team calls out what it sees, the commander turns it into one plan, and everyone pushes together.

HOW WE PLAY · 2/5

Hardcore settings

Kill cam Off

When you die, the game doesn't show who shot you or from where. The shooter stays hidden until someone calls out the position.

HUD Reduced

The game puts less on your screen. You check the map and listen to the calls to know what's going on.

Vehicle view 1st person

Drivers and gunners get no outside camera to look over cover. The players around a vehicle call what its crew can't see.

Destruction On

Explosives break walls and cover. The building you hold may not stand for long, so plan where to fall back.
