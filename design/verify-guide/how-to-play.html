HOW WE PLAY

One team.One plan.

Every faction gets a commander. Squads still fight their own fights, but everyone chases the same objective at the same time.

The zone scores on bodies inside it, not on kills. Thirty players pulling five ways lose to twenty pulling one way.

How a commander is picked

1

Put your hand up

Pick Commander in #roles. That puts you in the Commander Pool.

2

The bot asks

When a team has no commander, the bot asks someone on it. Pool first, then people in the team's voice channel, then the best rated. Nobody in the pool? It asks someone at random.

3

60 seconds to say yes

The offer comes by DM. Accept and you get the Commander role. Say no, or don't answer, and the next person is asked.

4

It changes hands

Leave, switch sides, or stay out of voice for 2 minutes and someone else takes over. /standdown hands it on any time. Every match starts fresh.

If you're the commander

Make the calls

Where to push, when to take the zone, when to hold, when to hit the towers. You're the only voice in the channel, so keep it short.

"Everyone on the zone. Now."

Draw it on the live map

Open wardogs.tech in your team's voice channel from the Activities button. Your arrows and markers show up live for everyone listening.

LIVE MAPwardogs.tech

Keep your head

Your team rates you after every match. Calm and clear gets you picked again. Yelling at people doesn't.

Everyone else

Listen in

Join your team's (listen) voice channel. Only the commander talks there, so every call is clear.

Move together

Stick with your squad. When the call comes, go. A late push is a lost push.

Report in TEAM chat

Say where, what, and which way it's going. For an exact spot, right-click the map, use Mark Coordinates [M] and paste it.
