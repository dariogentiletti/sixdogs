# Rules
color: red
Short version: play fair, be decent, and look out for the new people.

## 1. Be decent to each other.
Banter about the match is fine. Going after someone personally is not. No slurs, no harassment, no bigotry.

## 2. No cheating, no exploits.
If you find a bug that gives you an edge, tell an admin instead of using it.

## 3. Stay in your own comms.
Each faction has its own voice channel. Don't try to get into another faction's channel or pass on what you hear there. That one gets you banned.

## 4. Follow the commander strategy.
In the (listen) channels, only the commander can speak. Make sure to follow his strategy and report what you see in TEAM chat in game. You can vote how the commander performed at the end of the game with our bot.

## 5. Keep it clean.
Nothing NSFW, no spam, and no ads for other servers, in text or voice.

## 6. Admins have the final say.
If you think a call was wrong, message one of us privately and we'll talk it through.
