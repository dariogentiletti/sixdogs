# Link your Discord to your game account
color: gold
Two commands, once, and you're done. **The bot can only find you while you're actually in the game**, so start WARDOGS first and stay in it until you've finished.

## 1. Join the server
In WARDOGS: **Community servers**, then search **{{server.search}}**, or use **Join by ID** with `{{server.id}}`.

## 2. Type /verify
Here in this channel, while you're in the game:
`/verify` and your in-game name, exactly as it shows on the scoreboard.

A short code arrives as a **private message inside the game**. It lasts 10 minutes.

## 3. Type /confirm
`/confirm` and that code. That's it. You get the **Verified** role straight away, and from then on your team colour follows you whenever you play.
